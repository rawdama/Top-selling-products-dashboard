 #Top Selling Products
Overview
This project, titled "Top Selling Products," provides an analysis of sales performance for various products, focusing on identifying top sellers across different regions, representatives, and marketing campaigns. The dataset includes sales information from 2022 to 2023.

Goal
The primary goal of this project is to:

Identify and analyze the top-selling products based on sales amount.
Understand the impact of different marketing campaigns on product sales.
Provide insights into sales trends over time to inform future marketing strategies.
Data Description
The dataset contains the following columns:

Sale Date: The date when the sale occurred.
Product Name: The name of the product sold.
Sales Amount: The total sales amount for the transaction.
Region: The geographical area where the sale took place.
Sales Representative: The name of the representative responsible for the sale.
Sales Team: The team to which the sales representative belongs.
Marketing Campaign: The campaign under which the product was sold.
